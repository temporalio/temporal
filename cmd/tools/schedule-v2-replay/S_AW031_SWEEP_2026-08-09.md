# s-aw031 Schedule V1 versus CHASM replay sweep

## Executive summary

This sweep read schedule and workflow history from `s-aw031` and replayed the observed Schedule V1
external inputs against fresh, in-memory CHASM scheduler state machines. It did not update, pause,
signal, migrate, or delete a live schedule.

The sweep did **not** establish a broad Schedule V2 correctness regression. Of 60 replayable V1
history runs, 51 completed within the per-history budget: 16 matched, 22 differed only in observed
timing, and 13 were initially classified as significant. Nine high-cost histories timed out.

The most important first-pass finding is that 12 of the 13 significant cases share a replay-harness
completion-mapping limitation. They should not be treated as product bugs until that limitation is
fixed and the cases are rerun. Three cases also expose normalized `StartWorkflowExecution` request
differences that require value-level inspection. One case has a paired missing/duplicate occurrence
while preserving total starts and action count.

Production identifiers are not included here. Case references are stable, truncated hashes from
the redacted report. The raw history bundle and identity crosswalk remain outside the repository in
an owner-only temporary directory.

## Finding 1: observation-time differences

Twenty-two cases differed only in `action_time`. Across their 899 compared starts, the absolute
V1/CHASM observation-time delta had a median of 70 ms, a 95th percentile of 274 ms, and a maximum
of 667 ms.

V1 history exposes the completion time of the local activity that called
`StartWorkflowExecution`; it does not expose the exact instant at which V1 selected the action.
CHASM executes its generator at an exact virtual-clock deadline. The two timestamps therefore
measure different points in the operation.

First-pass triage:

- **Severity: Low.** The workflow IDs, request fields, and action counts match in these cases.
- **Product-bug likelihood: Low.** This is expected measurement skew unless a later comparison
  shows a different nominal occurrence or scheduled-time search attribute.
- **Confidence: High** that these 22 cases are not action-selection differences.

This is separate from the controlled-corpus `TemporalScheduledStartTime` precision difference
documented in [DIVERGENCES.md](DIVERGENCES.md).

## Finding 2: carried workflow completions are not mapped faithfully

Twelve significant cases contain all of the following:

- one or more `completion_before_start` findings;
- an `unapplied_completion` at the replay horizon;
- missing CHASM starts; and
- a lower CHASM action count.

Together they account for 2,295 missing-start findings. In the largest three-run chain, V1
recorded 443, 499, and 499 starts while CHASM recorded none for the corresponding runs.

The evidence points to a harness limitation rather than a demonstrated scheduler difference:

1. Migration initialization imports V1 `RunningWorkflows` into CHASM buffered starts, preserving
   each workflow ID and run ID.
2. The replay harness initializes its V1-to-CHASM workflow-ID map as empty.
3. That map is populated only after CHASM emits a new start during the current replay run.
4. With strict local-activity capture enabled, a completion for a workflow that was already
   running at the run boundary is held as `completion_before_start`, even though the corresponding
   migrated CHASM buffered start already exists.
5. Under `SKIP` or `BUFFER_ONE`, the unapplied completion can keep the invoker occupied. Later
   occurrences accumulate or are skipped, producing a cascade of missing starts and an action-count
   difference.

The relevant paths are `replayV1HistoryAgainstCHASM`, `resolveObservedWorkflowID`, and
`applyObservedWatchCompletion` in `history_replay_test.go`. The conversion that preserves carried
running workflow IDs is `convertRunningWorkflowsToBufferedStarts` in `migration/migration.go`.

First-pass triage:

- **Reported severity: Significant**, because the current oracle sees missing workflow IDs and
  action-count differences.
- **Product-bug likelihood: Low pending rerun.** The common causal chain is explainable by the
  replay oracle's missing initial identity mapping.
- **Confidence: High** that these cases are contaminated by the harness limitation.
- **Required determination:** seed the identity map from migrated running workflows, apply their
  observed completions by preserved workflow/run ID, and rerun these 12 cases. Only differences
  that survive that correction should be treated as V1/V2 behavioral candidates.

## Finding 3: start-request field differences

Three significant cases contain 99 normalized `StartWorkflowExecution` request mismatches. The
field-level counts are:

| Field | Mismatches | Possible impact |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Search attributes | 59 | Visibility queries and workflow code reading scheduled metadata may observe different values. |
| Last completion result | 36 | A workflow consuming the previous successful result may receive different input. |
| Continued failure | 14 | A workflow consuming the previous failure may observe different failure state. |

A single request can differ in more than one field, so these counts do not sum to 99.

The current redacted report records field names but not value-level diffs. The search-attribute
differences may be the same whole-second versus sub-second `TemporalScheduledStartTime` difference
confirmed in the controlled corpus, but this sweep alone does not prove that. The completion fields
deserve closer attention: V1 stores `LastCompletionResult` as `Payloads`, while the CHASM migration
state stores a single success payload plus an optional failure.

First-pass triage:

- **Severity: Low to Medium pending value-level inspection.** Search-attribute precision is likely
  low impact; a changed completion result or continued failure can affect workflow behavior.
- **Product-bug likelihood: Undetermined.** Two of the three cases also contain the carried-
  completion mapping problem, but request comparison occurs independently and should be retained.
- **Next evidence needed:** emit a payload-safe structural diff that identifies the search-
  attribute key and distinguishes absent, empty, truncated, and value-changed completion fields.
  Payload contents must remain in the restricted report.

## Finding 4: occurrence identity differences

Two cases contain isolated occurrence-identity findings:

- one case has one `duplicate_action` paired with one `missing_action`, while both implementations
  report 56 starts and an action count of 422;
- one case has one `extra_action`, three missing actions, and an action-count difference of two.

Both histories contain pause/update or start-failure interactions. The second also contains the
carried-completion mapping limitation. These could represent deadline-ordering behavior like the
controlled `SKIP` boundary case, but the present evidence does not isolate that cause.

First-pass triage:

- **Severity: Medium if confirmed**, because a different nominal occurrence changes the workflow
  ID even when total action count is unchanged.
- **Product-bug likelihood: Undetermined.** Rerun after fixing completion mapping, then build a
  timeline of the surviving extra/missing workflow IDs, pause/update signals, nominal deadlines,
  and observed completions.

## Non-comparisons and performance limits

### Missing initialization state

Thirty-one downloaded legacy-workflow candidates did not contain `Schedule` and `InternalState`
in the `WorkflowExecutionStarted` input. They are not V1/CHASM behavioral comparisons. They may be
sentinel workflows or otherwise insufficient history, and are excluded from the 60-run replayable
denominator.

The collector now validates decoded `StartScheduleArgs` before identifying a history as Schedule
V1. The replay path also returns `initial_state_unavailable` instead of panicking when such a file
is supplied manually. This was fixed in commit `8acdd06de`.

### Timeouts

Nine histories from one namespace exceeded the 15-second isolated replay budget. They are
performance/inconclusive cases, not mismatches. Their schedules generate enough virtual work that
a sparse wall-clock history can cause large numbers of CHASM tasks during time skipping.

Before a larger sweep, the harness should enforce a per-case virtual-action/task budget and report
`replay_budget_exceeded` with the simulated time range and generated-task count. This avoids both
unbounded test time and misclassifying a timeout as behavioral evidence.

## Overall determination

The sweep provides three useful conclusions:

1. Sixteen production histories matched and 22 more selected the same actions with only expected
   observation-time skew.
2. The bulk of the apparent severe divergence is not actionable until carried V1 workflow
   completions are mapped to the workflows already present in migrated CHASM state.
3. Start-request search attributes and completion-result/failure fields are the strongest
   remaining compatibility candidates and need restricted, value-level comparison.

No production-sweep result currently justifies changing Schedule V2 behavior. The next highest-
value step is to correct completion mapping, rerun the 12 affected cases, and then investigate any
surviving request or occurrence-identity difference against the controlled scenarios in
[DIVERGENCES.md](DIVERGENCES.md).
